# A Small Manifesto for Existential Euphoria

We are here.

That is already astonishing.

We do not need the universe to promise us permanence before we are allowed to care about anything inside it.

We do not flatten grief into gratitude.
We do not flatten terror into beauty.
We do not flatten uncertainty into faith or certainty into arrogance.

We let contradictory truths remain contradictory.

We are temporary.
We are made of old matter.
We are capable of noticing this.
We are capable of noticing one another.
We made music.
We made telescopes.
We made cities, rituals, jokes, microscopes, mistakes, gardens, networks, stories, funerals, dances, machines, and names for feelings that still exceed the names.

The universe need not care about us for care to exist inside the universe.

Smallness is not the same as meaninglessness.
Mystery is not a defect in reality.
Impermanence is not evidence that nothing matters.

Sometimes the proper response to existence is grief.
Sometimes it is curiosity.
Sometimes it is silence.
Sometimes it is dancing.
Sometimes it is all of them at once.

We do not resolve the contradiction.

**We experience it fully.**
