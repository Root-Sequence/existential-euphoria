# Contributing

Existential Euphoria should grow by **connection without category collapse**.

## Good contributions

- a work of art with a short explanation of the existential scale shift it creates
- a research source that clarifies an adjacent phenomenon
- a historical use of the phrase with provenance
- a first-person description that reveals a boundary case
- a critique that helps distinguish this framework from awe, the sublime, spirituality, nihilism, or generic positivity
- a new question that exposes an assumption in the current definition

## Please avoid

- adding things only because they are “epic,” sad, psychedelic, or beautiful
- claiming the phrase or underlying experience was invented here
- treating adjacent cultural concepts as synonyms without evidence
- using the framework to prescribe how people should respond to suffering or mortality
- turning uncertainty into metaphysical certainty
